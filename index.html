01 About

Learning from the world.

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. My path into AI began with mathematics at Sun Yat-sen University, followed by a master’s in artificial intelligence at the University of Hong Kong.

I work on embodied intelligence and multimodal large language models. I’m drawn to models that build their understanding through interaction: predicting what comes next, staying grounded in what they observe, and improving through experience.
